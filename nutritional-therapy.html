Nutritional Therapy

A Nutritional Therapy Practitioner provides a personalized, whole-body approach to health and wellness that focuses on nourishing the body, supporting its natural functions, and building a strong foundation for long-term well-being. Rather than taking a one-size-fits-all approach, nutritional therapy recognizes that every individual has unique needs, challenges, and goals. Nutritional therapy looks at how factors such as digestion, blood sugar balance, nutrient status, sleep, stress, hormones, and eating patterns may be influencing overall well-being.

Support may be especially helpful for individuals navigating concerns such as emotional eating, disordered eating patterns, low energy, digestive discomfort, blood sugar fluctuations, stress-related symptoms, and challenges with their relationship with food. Nutritional therapy works alongside medical and mental-health care when appropriate, with the goal of helping clients build sustainable habits and better support their body's foundational needs.

An NTP provides nutrition education and wellness support and does not diagnose, treat, or cure medical conditions. Nutritional therapy is intended to complement, not replace, care from licensed healthcare professionals.
