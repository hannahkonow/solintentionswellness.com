Recovery Coaching

REBT Addiction Recovery helps support healthier thinking, manage emotional distress, and change self-defeating behaviors.

Benefits of Abstaining:

- Practicing self care

- Reduction of anxiety

- Improved sleep

- Improved relationships

- Clearer thinking

- Rebuilding trust

- Enhanced self esteem

- Setting & achieving goals

A Recovery Coach assists you in facilitating positive steps towards desired change while taking the actions necessary to achieve abstinence. A trained non-clinical peer professional provides personalized support, structure, and accountability for individuals navigating early recovery or long-term sobriety. Coaching is not therapy, not detox, and not a substitute for a sponsor or recovery community (it can complement both). It is not emergency/crisis services. If you are in immediate danger, call 911.
